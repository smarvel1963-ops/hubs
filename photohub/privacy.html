Photo Hub privacy policy

Photo Hub is an app by Marvel Corp. Last updated November 25, 2026.

The short version

Your photos and videos are yours. Photo Hub shows and sorts them on your phone. Nothing leaves your phone unless you turn on Cloud Backup or link your own PC. There are no ads, no tracking and no selling of data, ever.

What stays on your phone

  • Your photo library. Photo Hub reads your photos and videos to show them. It never changes them. Only when you use Clean Up or Duplicate Cleanup and say yes - and then say yes again in Android's own question - does it move phone copies to your phone's trash, where Android keeps them about 30 days. Clean Up only offers photos it has just checked are safe in your cloud backup; Duplicate Cleanup only offers exact copies and always leaves one. Your cloud backups are never touched by either.
  • Albums, tags, hearts and settings you make in the app.
  • Notifications. "Backup finished" and "On this day" notes are made on your phone from your own photos' dates. You can turn each one off in the app or in Android's settings.
  • Events. Your big days are found from your photos' own dates, on your phone.
  • Where photos were taken. Places and Trips read the location saved inside each photo and names the town using a list of towns built into the app. That happens on your phone; no location is sent anywhere for it.
  • The Private Vault. Photos you put in the Vault are locked on your phone with your code. They are never uploaded.

What leaves your phone, and why

WhatWhenWhere it goes
Your name, email address and Google account IDOnly if you sign in with Google (needed for Cloud Backup) Google's sign-in service (Firebase Authentication) and Marvel Corp's backup service, to know whose backup is whose
Copies of your photos and videos, with what is saved inside them (such as the date, the camera and where they were taken), plus your album names and which photos are in each album Only if you turn on Cloud Backup Marvel Corp's backup service (run on Cloudflare) stores them in a private storage bucket at Backblaze in the United States. They travel encrypted (HTTPS) and are stored encrypted (AES-256). Each person's backup is kept apart, and only you can reach it.
Copies of your photosOnly if you link your own PC Your own computer: over your home Wi-Fi, or from your cloud backup with a read-only link code you create and can remove.

Backblaze and Cloudflare store and carry the data for us as service providers. They do not use it for anything else. We do not share your data with anyone else.

Delete your backups and account

To delete your cloud backups and your Photo Hub account, email smarvel1963@gmail.com from the Google account you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Photo Hub account". We delete your backed-up photos, videos, albums, PC links and your sign-in record (name, email address and Google account ID), and confirm by email. Deleted files are gone for good 30 days later (storage keeps them that long in case of a mistake). The photos on your phone are not touched. You can also open this page from Photo Hub: Cloud, then "Delete my account and backups".

To remove Photo Hub from your phone and everything it keeps there, uninstall the app.

Children

Photo Hub is not made for children under 13.

Selling data

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use your photos to train anything.

Changes

If what leaves your phone changes, this page changes first, with a new date at the top.
